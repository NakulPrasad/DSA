## 📝 Problem Statement

### Examples
```text
Input: {1, 5, 3, 19, 18, 25}
Output: 1
Explanation: Minimum difference is between 18 and 19

Input: {30, 5, 20, 9}
Output: 4
Explanation: Minimum difference is between 5 and 9

Input: {1, 19, -4, 31, 38, 25, 100}
Output: 5
Explanation: Minimum difference is between 1 and -4
```
